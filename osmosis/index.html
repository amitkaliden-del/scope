    The question and your prediction

    What will happen to onion cells in a strong salt solution?

    You will compare a CONTROL (onion in water) with a TEST (onion in salt water). Predict first, then look. Your prediction does not need to be right, it needs a reason.

    What do onion epidermal cells look like?

    A real photo of onion epidermis at 40×. Look at the shape of the cells, the dark cell walls, and how the cells fit together.

    Each cell has a cell wall around it. Inside is the cytoplasm and a nucleus. Salt solution is more concentrated than the cell contents.
