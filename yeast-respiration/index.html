    The question

    How does temperature affect the rate of respiration in yeast?

    Yeast is a tiny living fungus. It breaks down glucose and gives off carbon dioxide (CO₂). In the UAE, bakeries use yeast to make khubz and other breads, and bakers control the temperature of the dough so the yeast works well. You will measure the CO₂ the yeast makes at different temperatures.

    Variables

    • Change (independent): temperature of the water bath
    • Measure (dependent): rate of CO₂ production
    • Keep the same (controlled): volume of yeast and glucose, same recipe, same time, oil layer (yes or no) for every tube, time to equilibrate

    The numbers in this simulation come from a model of typical yeast behaviour. They are not measured data.
