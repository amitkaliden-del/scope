Question Board: Privacy

Question Board is a classroom tool for Grade 10 Biology (Unit 6, The Human Body) at Emirates National Schools, MBZ Campus, Abu Dhabi. Students post questions to their class wall.

What we collect

Your Google account email and Google user ID (from Sign in with Google), your class, your first name, your student ID, and the questions you post.

Why

To show your name and class on your posts, to let your teacher check flagged posts, and to stop one person using two accounts. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell or share it.

Who can see it

Your classmates see your first name, student ID and posted questions. Your teacher also sees your email and any held or flagged posts.

How long we keep it

Until the end of the unit. Your teacher exports the posts for class use, then clears the board. Ask your teacher to remove your setup at any time.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (Workers and Durable Objects). Your Google password is never seen or stored.

Questions

Ask your Biology teacher at school.
